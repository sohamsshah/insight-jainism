Charts below are built from the [24 Tirthankars](/docs/category/24-tirthankars) biography data and taking reference from [Triloksar scripture](/docs/category/triloksar).

## Life spans

Life span decreases from the first to the 24th Tirthankar. **All values are in years** — the first ten (originally in Lakh Purva) are converted using 1 Purva = 70,560,000,000,000 years. The chart uses a log scale so the full range is visible.

## Time between Consecutive Tirthankars

Gap in time from one Tirthankar to the next. Early gaps are in Jain innumerable units (Saagar, Kodi Saagar, Palya); later gaps are in years.

Palya and Saagar are innumerable units of measurement. 
Total time for [4th Era](/docs/Insights/Kaal_Chakra) (when tirthankars are born) is 1 Koda-kodi Saagar.
- 1 Saagar = Innumerable Palyas
- 1 Palya = Innumerable years

Read more about [Absolute Numeral System](/docs/category/absolute-numeral-system) of Jainism.

## Height (in numbers)

Heights are in **Dhanush** for the first 22 Tirthankars and in **Haath** for the last two (Parshwanath and Mahavir Swami).

## Area of Samavsaran

The Area of Samavsaran decreases from 12 Yojan (Aadinath) to 1 Yojan (Mahavir Swami), in steps of 0.5 Yojan (with 1.25 Yojan for Parshwanath).
